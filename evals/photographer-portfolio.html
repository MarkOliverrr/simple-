01 — about

june park

Seoul, South Korea — Freelance Photographer

I document the tension between structure and atmosphere. My practice focuses on the geometric lines of Seoul's brutalist architecture and the silent transitions of light across the urban landscape at night.

Working exclusively with high-contrast monochrome and natural night-time illumination, I attempt to capture the quiet, editorial solitude that exists beneath the city's frantic pace.
